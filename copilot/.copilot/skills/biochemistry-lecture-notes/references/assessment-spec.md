# Assessment Package Specification

All questions must test material that is actually taught in the completed unit.
Use the official unit CO and label every item with its CO and Bloom level in
both student and teacher-facing documents.

Use Bloom levels BL1 through BL4 as appropriate. Do not use BL5 or BL6 unless
the user explicitly changes this requirement.

## Subjective Questions

- Include exactly 10 subjective questions at the end of the student notes.
- Every question carries 2 marks.
- Questions should require concise, medium-length answers rather than one-word
  recall or long essays.
- Distribute questions across BL1–BL4 in a way appropriate to the unit.
- Write the tag in this form:

  ```text
  [COx | BLn | 2 marks]
  ```

## Teacher Key for Subjective Questions

For every subjective question provide:

- a brief acceptable answer;
- two to four essential marking points;
- tolerance for equivalent scientifically correct wording.

Do not put the answers in the student notes.

## Quiz

Create a separate 10-mark quiz paper containing exactly:

- 7 basic one-best-answer MCQs;
- 2 higher-order-thinking MCQs;
- 1 assertion–reason MCQ.

## Surprise Test

Create a distinct 10-mark surprise-test paper using the same pattern:

- 7 basic one-best-answer MCQs;
- 2 higher-order-thinking MCQs;
- 1 assertion–reason MCQ.

The surprise test must not simply reorder or lightly reword the quiz.

## MCQ Construction Rules

- Use four options and exactly one best answer.
- Use plausible distractors based on likely student misconceptions.
- Avoid trick wording, double negatives, and irrelevant trivia.
- Do not use "all of the above" or "none of the above".
- Keep the default difficulty accessible, with only the two designated HOTS
  items substantially more analytical.
- Use a standard four-option assertion–reason key.
- Display CO and BL tags on every item.
- Keep answers and one-sentence rationales only in the teacher key.

## Default Assessment Blueprint for a Five-Hour Introductory Unit

| Assessment | BL1 | BL2 | BL3 | BL4 | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Subjective questions | 2 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 10 |
| Quiz | 3 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 10 |
| Surprise test | 3 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 10 |

Adapt the distribution when the official unit outcome clearly requires it, but
preserve the total item counts and the BL1–BL4 boundary.
